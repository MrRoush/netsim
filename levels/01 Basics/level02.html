In this level, you will create a packet and send it from one computer to another.

Pause the simulation and click on the packet that is sent from Bob's computer to Alice's computer. A packet's headers contain information used to deliver it. This is a simple packet that only lists information in the network layer. Note the two pieces of information contained in this header: srcip (the source of the packet) and dstip (the destination of the packet).

Level Objectives

To pass this level, you will need to send a packet from Alice to Bob. To do this:

  • Click + to add a packet and choose Alice as its sender.
  • In the network layer, set srcip to Alice and dstip to Bob.
    Hint: pause the simulation and inspect Bob's packet to see how these fields are written.
  • Click Add, restart, and click the send arrow beside your packet.
