A home network may use network address translation (NAT) at its router to let several devices share an outside address. In this simplified activity, the device named Home changes outgoing packet addresses and remembers where replies should go. A modem provides the connection to an internet service provider; it is not the same thing as NAT. Pause and compare a packet before and after it passes through Home.

To complete the level, send an ICMP ping from Alice to Google and watch the reply return.

Remember: set the transport-layer proto field to ICMP.

Level Objectives
