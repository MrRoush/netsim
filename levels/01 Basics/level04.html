Computers may be connected through one or more routers. A router reads a packet's destination address and uses its routing table to choose the next link. It does not need a packet to list every router along the way.

Pause the simulation and follow a packet from Bob to Carol. The destination stays Carol as each router makes its own forwarding decision. This activity uses simple, pre-set routes; real networks can choose routes using more detailed rules.

To complete the level, create and send a packet from Bob to Carol.

Level Objectives

  • Send a packet from Bob to Carol.
