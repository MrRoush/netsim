Welcome! This level introduces the simulator controls.

Use this level to get used to the simulator interface.

Click a device to see its name and address. Click pause, then click a moving packet to inspect its source and destination.

Pausing gives you time to inspect what is happening. Use restart to replay the activity from the beginning.

Follow the steps below to complete this level.

Level Objectives

  • Click restart in the top left.
  • Pause the simulation.
  • Click a device, then inspect a packet.
  • Click + to add a packet. You can leave its fields blank for this practice step.
  • Click the send arrow beside your packet.
