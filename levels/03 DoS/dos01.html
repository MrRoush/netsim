A denial-of-service (DoS) attack sends so much traffic to a computer or server that it cannot respond normally to other users. The meter in this activity is a simple model of a server's capacity, not a measurement of a real website.

In this level, send enough packets to fill Google's meter. Experiment with the Repeat setting and observe how quickly the meter rises and falls. In real life, DoS attacks disrupt access and cause harm; this simulation is for understanding defenses.

Hint: Use the packet editor's Repeat setting to send several copies.

Level Objectives

  • Fill Google's capacity meter by sending repeated packets.
