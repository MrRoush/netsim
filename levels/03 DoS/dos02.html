A firewall checks traffic against rules and can block packets from a listed source. Here, the firewall blocks Alice, so her packets do not reach Google. This activity uses one simple source-address rule; real firewalls can check many kinds of traffic.

A distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attack sends traffic from many devices. A firewall that blocks one source may not stop traffic from other sources. In this simulation, send traffic from the additional computers to Google.

Level Objectives

  • Send enough packets from the additional computers to fill Google's capacity meter.
