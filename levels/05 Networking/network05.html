A VLAN (virtual LAN) separates devices into groups on a switch. Devices in different VLANs are isolated at the switch unless a router is configured to connect those groups. Here, Alice and Bob are in VLAN 10; Guest and Teacher are in VLAN 20.

The VLAN labels are configured on the switch ports and are hidden from the packet editor. The simulation focuses on which traffic the switch forwards; it does not model Ethernet VLAN tags or inter-VLAN routing.

Level Objectives

  • Send a packet from Alice to Bob in the same VLAN.
  • Try to send a packet from Alice to Guest in another VLAN and observe that it stops at the switch.
