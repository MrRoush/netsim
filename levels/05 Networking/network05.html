A VLAN (virtual local area network) divides devices connected to a switch into separate logical groups. Devices in the same VLAN can communicate through the switch; devices in different VLANs cannot communicate unless a router connects their groups. Alice and Bob are in VLAN 10; Guest and Teacher are in VLAN 20. The VLAN membership is preconfigured on the switch's ports and is not a packet field you need to enter. This simulation does not model Ethernet VLAN tags or inter-VLAN routing.

For each packet, set Network srcip to Alice and set the destination in dstip. Leave the Transport and Application fields blank.

  1. Set Network dstip to Bob and send the packet. Alice and Bob are both in VLAN 10, so it arrives.
  2. Change Network dstip to Guest and send another packet. Guest is in VLAN 20, so the switch does not forward it.

Level Objectives

  • Compare packet delivery to Bob and Guest, who are in different VLANs.
