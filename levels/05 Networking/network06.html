A key is information used by encryption systems; a device must verify that a key belongs to the person it expects before trusting it. This activity demonstrates that check with text labels only. It does not perform encryption or verify identities using certificates or digital signatures.

No packet entry is needed: the two key replies are sent automatically. Pause and click each reply to inspect its Application fields. Eve's reply claims Network srcip Bob, but its key is Eve's key. Alice ignores it. Bob's reply has the expected key trusted:Bob, so Alice automatically sends a verified-message to Bob.

Level Objectives

  • Compare the two key labels and observe which reply Alice accepts.
