The srcip (claimed sender) and dstip (destination) fields can be edited. A packet can claim to come from Carol even when Alice actually sent it. This is called IP spoofing. The simulation shows why relying only on a claimed source address can be unsafe; real networks use additional checks to verify traffic, and spoofing does not give someone permission to use another device.

Send a packet from Alice to Bob with srcip set to Carol.

Observe the difference between the device that sent the packet and the sender named in its header.

Level Objectives

  • Make Bob receive a packet that claims to come from Carol.
