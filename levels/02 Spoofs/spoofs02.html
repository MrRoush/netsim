This level connects several devices to a switch. A switch learns which connected link is associated with a device and uses that information to forward traffic. Unlike a router, a switch normally connects devices within a local network. This activity uses simplified address fields to show the learning idea; real Ethernet switches learn MAC addresses.

Your goal is to redirect a packet intended for Charlie so that it reaches Alice. Watch how the switch updates its learned information, then use the packet editor and timing to affect where the packet goes.

Hint: Timing matters. Pause, restart, and try different launch times to see what the switch has learned.

Level Objectives

  • Use Alice to redirect a packet sent from Google to Charlie.
